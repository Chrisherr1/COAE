## Now Whats Machine Learning then?..

`Machine Learning` is a sibfield of AI that focsues on enabling systems to learn from data and improve their preformance on specific task without explicit programming.

> They use Algroithmns to see patterns within datasets, allowing the system to make predications and decisions based on new data given.

3 Main types of ML:

`Supervised Learning` - Learns from labeled data to see what to look for. Used to Spam detection.

`Unsupervised Learning` - Learns from unlabeled data without providing the outcome or label. Used in Anomaly detection.

`Reinforcement Learning` - Learns through trial and error by interacting with environment and receiving rewards or pentalities.
Used in Robotics and Autonomous Driving.

> It is the enabler for AI to work. It provides the ability to learn and adapt.

---

## So whats Deep Learning?..

`Deep Learning (DL)` is a subfield of ML that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn and extract features from complex data.

> Neural networks can automatically identify patterns within large datasets making them powerful in unstructured data.

Key Features:

Hierarchical Feature Learning: They can break up the data into abstractions.

> For example, lower layers might detect edges and textures in image recognition, while higher layers identify more complex structures like shapes and objects.

They can be trained end to end meaning it can be trained with raw data and have it give desired output easily.

They can also be scalable with large datasets.

Common Neral networks:

`Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)`: used in image and video.
> Detect local patterns and spatial hiearchies.

`Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)`: used in text and speech.
> Allow information to persist across time steps

`Transformers`: Effective to natural language processing tasks.
> Self Attention mechanisms to handle Long-range dependencies.
